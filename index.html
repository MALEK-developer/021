AQUA SERVICES & ENGINEERING

Providing sustainable treatment solutions for municipal, mining industry and hospitality!
Aqua Services & Engineering is the leading Namibian supplier and service partner for reagents, chemicals, equipment and components required for water & wastewater treatment, mining extractions, industry and hospitality environment. ASE’s extensive expertise in the application of reagents and chemicals, backed by an international supplier network and substantial local stockholding capacity, provides ASE client’s the reliance of continued supply and application optimization, thus preserving our pristine environment.
The professional engineering team of ASE develops state-of-the-art solutions for water and wastewater treatment and associated processes, based on experience gained out of the basket applied technologies. An assortment of selected global manufacturers is being represented in their advanced technology applications. To ensure the sustainable operation of water and wastewater systems, ASE provides extensive back-up in maintenance services and full on-site plant operations to ensure the reliable supply of treated water and wastewater, thus ensuring reuse of our scarce resources and protecting the environment. An extensive basket of advanced products, serviced by our trained technicians, including advanced pre-paid water metering systems, enable our customers to provide accurate and safe water to consumers.
